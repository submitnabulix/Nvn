Submission Guidelines & FAQ

Everything you need to know before and after you submit.

Submission checklist
Audio file ready (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC or OGG, up to 50 MB) • Official lyrics • Correct artist name and credits • Rights confirmed for music, lyrics and artwork

Before you submit

Is there a fee to submit?

No. Creating an account and submitting a song is free.

Which audio files are supported?

MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC and OGG, up to 50 MB. WAV or FLAC gives the best result, or MP3 at 320 kbps.

What cover art can I upload?

Cover art is optional. Use a JPG, PNG or WebP image under 5 MB that you own or are licensed to use.

Do I need to own the song?

Yes. You must own or control the rights to the music, lyrics and cover art. See our Copyright & Takedown Policy for details.

Which genres do you feature?

We focus on Brazilian Funk, Phonk, Montagem, Pop, K-pop and Electronic music. Other genres are welcome and are reviewed individually.

Review and decision

How long does the review take?

The NVN team usually responds within 3–5 days. This is an estimate, and busy periods may take longer.

How will I know the result?

The status updates on your dashboard and you receive a notification with a message from the team.

What if my song is declined?

Read the team’s message, improve your song, and use “Edit & resubmit” on your dashboard to submit it again.

After acceptance

What happens if my song is accepted?

We produce a lyric video and publish it on the NVN Music YouTube channel.

Can I ask for my video to be removed?

Yes. Email suport.nvn@gmail.com from your account email address and we will remove it.

Account and privacy

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose “Forgot password?” on the login page and follow the link sent to your email address.

How is my data used?

Please read our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Copyright & Takedown Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

NVN Music respects the rights of creators and rights holders and responds promptly to valid notices of alleged infringement.

1. Submitting content

Only submit songs, lyrics and cover art that you own or have permission to use. Every submission requires you to confirm this.

2. Reporting infringement

If you believe content on NVN Music or on our YouTube channel infringes your rights, email suport.nvn@gmail.com with:

  • your full name and contact details;
  • a description of the work you own and proof of ownership;
  • the URL or title of the content you believe infringes;
  • a statement that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them, and that the information you provide is accurate.

3. How we respond

We review each notice and may remove or disable access to the content while we investigate. We may share the notice with the person who submitted the content so they can respond.

4. Objections

If your content was removed and you believe this was a mistake, email us with your account details and evidence of your rights. We will review it in good faith.

5. Repeat infringement

Accounts that repeatedly submit infringing content, or that make knowingly false claims, may be suspended or closed.

6. Artist removal requests

If your song was accepted and you would like the lyric video removed, email us from your account email address and we will take it down.

Content Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy sets out what may be submitted to NVN Music. The NVN team applies it to every submission and to published content.

1. Not permitted

  • Content you do not own or have permission to use.
  • Hate speech, harassment, threats or content that promotes violence.
  • Sexual content involving minors, or other illegal content.
  • Content that exposes private information or impersonates another person or artist.
  • Spam, misleading credits, or repeated duplicate submissions.

2. Lyrics and artwork

Lyrics must match the submitted song. Cover art must be your own work or properly licensed and must not use others’ logos or trademarks without permission.

3. Accuracy

Artist names, credits and links must be accurate. Do not submit under another person’s name.

4. Enforcement

The NVN team may decline a submission, remove published content, or suspend an account that breaches this policy. Decisions are made in good faith, and you can contact us at suport.nvn@gmail.com to ask for a review.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information NVN Music, a brand of NVN Social Groups (“NVN”, “we”, “us”), collects when you use this platform, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to operate the platform:

  • Account details: name or artist name, email address, country, account type, and optionally a phone or WhatsApp number, a social media link and a short biography.
  • Submission content: audio files, cover art, lyrics, credits, streaming links, release dates and notes you provide for the review team.
  • Communications: messages and file attachments you exchange with the NVN team through the Messages feature, decisions on your submissions, and emails you send to us.
  • Technical data: a login session stored in your browser, and basic technical information (such as IP address) processed by our service providers when you load or use the site.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you.
  • To review your submissions and communicate decisions to you.
  • To produce and publish a lyric video if your song is accepted.
  • To respond to support, copyright and privacy requests.
  • To protect the platform against misuse, fraud and unauthorised access, and to meet legal obligations.

3. What becomes public

Your account and unpublished submissions are not public. If a song is accepted, the song, artist name, lyrics and related credits may appear in a lyric video on the NVN Music YouTube channel. Content published on YouTube is also subject to YouTube’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the platform, and when required by law:

  • Supabase for authentication, database and file storage.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr to deliver fonts and code libraries, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • YouTube when an accepted song is published.

5. Data security

Your data is stored in a managed database with row-level security. Uploaded audio and cover files are kept in private storage, accessible only to you and authorised NVN team members. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong, unique password.

6. Retention

We keep your account information and submissions while your account is active and for as long as needed to maintain our records, handle disputes and comply with legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain uses of it, by contacting us at suport.nvn@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children

NVN Music is intended for users aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. By using the platform you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows when it last changed. Continued use of the platform after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to suport.nvn@gmail.com.

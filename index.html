Privacy Policy

NVN VERSE is built around visual storytelling — everything you upload is meant to be shared. This policy explains what we collect, how it's used, and the controls you have over your own content and data. Last updated: August 2026.

Information We Collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, username, and profile photo/banner if you add one.
  • Content you upload — verse images, titles, descriptions, and categories you choose to publish.
  • Activity data — likes, saves, follows, searches, and the images you view, so your feed stays relevant to you.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical information (app version, general performance data) used only to keep NVN VERSE stable and secure.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run the core NVN VERSE experience — displaying your uploads, powering search and "More From You" recommendations, keeping your saved and liked verses in sync across devices, and letting other users discover and follow your profile. We never use your personal data to sell ads to third parties.

Content Visibility & Your Control

Every verse you upload is visible to the community by default. You're always in control of what stays public — hide any upload at any time from your profile, and it's instantly removed from the home feed and search while staying safely in your account.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. Your public profile (username, uploads, follower count) is visible to other users by design, since NVN VERSE is a community platform. Account data is only shared with trusted infrastructure providers (such as our authentication and storage provider) strictly to operate the app.

Data Retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete a verse, it's removed from our systems shortly after. If you'd like your account and all associated data permanently removed, contact our support team and we'll process the request.

Your Rights & Choices

You can access, update, or delete your profile information at any time from Edit Profile, hide or delete individual uploads whenever you like, and opt out of non-essential notifications from the Notifications settings page.

Children's Privacy

NVN VERSE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as NVN VERSE evolves. We'll always reflect the latest version here, so it's worth checking back occasionally.

Contact Us

Questions about your privacy or data? We're happy to help.

Security

Keeping your account safe is a shared responsibility — here's what NVN VERSE does on our end, and a few quick actions you can take on yours.

Account Protection

Your login is handled through an encrypted, industry-standard authentication system. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they're hashed and secured, and all traffic between your device and our servers is protected with SSL/TLS encryption.

Change Your Password

We'll email a secure reset link straight to your account's email address.

Saved Accounts on This Device

NVN VERSE lets you keep up to 5 accounts saved on one device and switch between them instantly, without re-entering a password. Review or remove saved accounts anytime.

Two-Factor Authentication

We're working on 2FA support to give your account an extra layer of protection. It'll appear here as soon as it ships.

Staying Safe

  • Use a unique password you don't reuse on other apps.
  • Never share your password or reset link with anyone.
  • Log out of "My Accounts" on shared or public devices.
  • Watch for emails claiming to be NVN VERSE asking for your password — we'll never ask for it directly.

Reporting a Security Issue

Spotted a vulnerability or suspicious activity on your account? Let our team know right away.

About

Our Story

NVN VERSE started as a simple way to share images and has grown into a full creative platform. What began as a basic gallery is now a place where anyone can upload, discover, and curate visual content across a growing set of categories.

What We Do

NVN VERSE gives you a home for high-quality wallpapers, aesthetics, and everyday visual inspiration. Upload your own verses, follow creators whose taste matches yours, save what inspires you, and explore a feed shaped by what you actually engage with.

What You Can Do Here

  • Upload and organize your own verses by category.
  • Follow creators and build your own audience.
  • Save, like, and revisit your favorite finds.
  • Search and discover content matched to your taste.
  • Manage multiple accounts from one device.

Our Values

We're built on three principles: creativity should be easy to share, your privacy and control over your own content always come first, and this platform grows through its community — not around it.
